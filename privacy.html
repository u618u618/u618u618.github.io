English

This policy explains what information Raindrop Race (“the game”), made by U618 (“we”), handles, and the choices you have.

1. Information the game collects

The game itself does not collect, store on a server, or share your personal information. Your progress (S balance, cars, stats, collection and settings) is saved only on your device and is never sent to us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • device identifiers (Apple IDFA or Android Advertising ID, if you allow it), IP address and approximate location;
  • information about ads shown and your interactions with them;
  • device and app information and diagnostics.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your choices

  • iPhone / iPad: the game asks for permission before allowing tracking. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • In regions where it is required (such as the EEA and the UK), a consent form is shown before ads.
  • Ads are optional: you only see one when you choose to watch it to revive.

4. Feedback email

If you send us feedback by email, we receive your email address and whatever you include (the game pre-fills the version, device model and number of races). We use it only to reply and fix problems, and we do not share it.

5. Deleting your data

Use Settings › Reset save in the game, or delete the app. Both remove all game data from your device. We hold no copy.

6. Age

The game contains simulated gambling and is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Virtual score

S is a virtual score with no monetary value. It cannot be purchased or exchanged for money, prizes or anything of value.

8. Changes and contact

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with a new effective date. Questions: oolong19530303@gmail.com
